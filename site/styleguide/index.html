06 · Accessibility

Contrast (WCAG 2.2 AA) applied

Normal text needs a contrast ratio of 4.5:1, and large text (24px+, or 18.66px+ bold) needs 3:1. Fixes are scoped to the elements that failed, so the same colors are unchanged where they already passed (for example, on dark backgrounds).

End‑to‑end container transport across Canada

Carmel Transport provides complete shipping container transport services for import, export and domestic moves anywhere in Canada. From CN and CP rail terminals to shipper warehouses and storage yards, we plan and execute every leg of the move so you do not have to coordinate multiple vendors.

Why Shippers
Choose Carmel Transport

For shippers moving containers every day, the right partner keeps boxes flowing instead of sitting at the terminal. Our shipping container moving services are built to do exactly that.

Faster container turn‑around

We focus on reducing the time your shipping containers spend at ports and rail terminals. Efficient scheduling and dispatch mean quicker pickups, faster delivery to your site, and less risk of demurrage or detention fees.

Purpose‑built equipment for containers

Our fleet is set up specifically for shipping container moving: dedicated container chassis, experienced drivers and the right permits for 20‑, 40‑, 45‑ and 53‑foot units. That lets us move your boxes safely and consistently, day after day.

Consistent communication on every move

From booking to final drop‑off, our team keeps you updated on each container move with clear ETAs and status updates. You always know where your box is, when the truck will arrive, and what’s happening next.

Planning that protects your margins

We design each container drayage move around rail cutoffs, terminal appointments, and empty returns, helping you reduce detention, demurrage, and unnecessary storage costs while keeping freight flowing.

02

Reliable coordination across GTA terminals

Our operations team manages import and export container activity through key GTA terminals, so your container transport runs with fewer delays, missed cutoffs, and costly last‑minute changes.

03

Stronger control over rail timelines

By securing CN and CP rail reservations in advance, we help you stay ahead of container availability and critical export cutoffs, aligning drayage, yard, and rail schedules in one plan.

04

Visibility that keeps shipments moving

Daily monitoring across CN, CP, and other terminals gives your team clear visibility into container status and ETA, making it easier to react quickly when conditions change.

05

Support that goes beyond the truck move

From pickup at the terminal to final delivery and empty return, we coordinate the full container flow with 24/7 customer service and secured export handling with high‑security bolt seals.

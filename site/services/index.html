Refrigerated Container Transport and Storage

Carmel offers refrigerated container transport and storage for temperature-sensitive cargo that needs reliable control in transit and at the yard. Our fleet and chassis capability support reefer movements for perishable and protected freight.

  • Refrigerated container transport for temperature-sensitive freight
  • Temperature transport solutions for food and specialty cargo
  • Reefer storage support at secure yard locations

Secure Container Storage Facility

Our secure container storage facility supports dry-box, refrigerated and in-bond containers for short-term and long-term requirements. Strategically located yards near rail terminals in Mississauga help simplify overflow, staging and timing challenges.

  • Shipping container storage facility for dry and reefer units
  • Secure yard storage close to major rail terminals
  • Short-term and long-term container storage options

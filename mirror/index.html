Built Around Drayage Transportation

Container trucking company you can rely on

We are a dedicated container trucking company specializing in the transport of shipping containers. With the right equipment, reliable chassis, and experienced drivers, we have the capacity to handle your container transport needs efficiently and safely.

Secure Container Storage

Whether for daily shipments or long-term storage, in our secured yards your freight remains safe while awaiting rail, transloading, or final delivery.

Safety First

With Canada Customs bonded status and C‑TPAT and PIP certifications, Carmel Transport upholds the highest standards of safety and compliance. Our in-house programs and round-the-clock dispatch ensure seamless, worry-free drayage logistics.
